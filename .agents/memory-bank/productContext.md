# Product Context & Requirements

## Problem Statement
School environments impacted by violence, insecurity, and poor relational climate force students and teachers to dedicate cognitive and emotional resources to stress management, potentially hindering learning performance.

## Target Audience / Scope
- Monograph in Statistics and Quantitative Modeling.
- Geographic Scope: Southern Region of Brazil (PR: 41, SC: 42, RS: 43).
- Target Population: 3rd and 4th grade High School (Ensino Médio) students participating in SAEB 2023.

## Research Questions & Hypotheses
- **H1**: Student perception of unwholesome school environment is negatively associated with academic performance after controlling for INSE and school factors.
- **H2**: Aggregated school teacher violence index exerts an incremental negative effect on performance.
- **H3**: The student environment effect varies across schools (random slope), indicating moderation by institutional variables.
